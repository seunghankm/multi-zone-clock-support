Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Multi-Zone Clock helps you compare time zones, set alarms, and manage calendars. This policy explains what stays on your device and what is sent when you use online features.

Who operates the app

Multi-Zone Clock is an independent app made by Han Kim (seunghankm), who is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. For support or privacy requests, contact pureunhouse@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Saved time zones and labels, alarms and imported alarm sounds, local calendar events, holiday notes and alerts, preferences, and unsent feedback drafts are stored on your device. Some data is shared with the app’s widget extension through an on-device shared container. Connected calendar data and pending changes are also cached locally.

We do not automatically upload these local records to a developer-operated calendar service. Your device’s backup and restore settings may include app data. Data you explicitly send as feedback is handled separately below.

Notifications and alarms

Notification and alarm permissions are used only to ring alarms and show reminders on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is on by default and can be turned off in the app’s Settings. While it’s on, your local calendars and events, holiday countries and notes, time zones and labels, connected Apple calendars (their account and calendar names, with your descriptions and icons), and published calendar links are kept in your own private iCloud database through Apple’s CloudKit, so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account have them too. Each record’s contents are stored in CloudKit’s encrypted fields, which we cannot read; only its type, a format version, when it last changed, whether it was deleted and, for a time zone or country, its name (the time zone or country code) are stored outside them. Under Apple’s standard data protection, Apple holds the keys to these fields. If you turn on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud, they are end-to-end encrypted and only your trusted devices can decrypt them. This data is not sent to a developer-operated service. It counts toward your iCloud storage.

Alarms, preferences, and which calendars and alerts you turn off on a device stay on that device. Turning iCloud Sync off stops sending changes and deletes nothing. To remove the data from iCloud, manage this app’s data in your Apple Account’s iCloud storage settings.

Apple Calendar access

Calendar access is optional. If you grant access, the app can discover system calendars; you choose which ones to connect. The app reads and writes events in those selected calendars through Apple’s EventKit framework. When you move a local calendar to Apple Calendar, its supported events are saved to the selected system calendar. Apple and your calendar account provider handle account synchronization and related notifications.

Icons, app-specific descriptions, and View In display preferences stay in this app, and with iCloud Sync on also in your private iCloud data. Disconnecting a calendar removes its app connection and cache; it does not delete the calendar or its events from Apple Calendar. Unresolved writes may need recovery before disconnecting.

Optional calendar subscription links

In development builds that offer subscriptions, you may explicitly publish a local calendar as a read-only feed. This uploads its name, color, event titles, times, time zones, repeat rules, locations, notes and URLs to our Cloudflare Worker and D1 service. The feed contains no event alerts; this app continues scheduling local alerts. Apple Calendar or another subscriber may apply its own alert settings.

Anyone who has the subscription link can read the published calendar. Treat the link as a secret. Transport is encrypted with HTTPS, but the stored feed is not end-to-end encrypted and can be processed by the hosting service. Do not publish information you do not want link holders to see. We do not use calendar contents for advertising or sell them.

Feeds update when this app publishes committed changes while running with a connection. Subscriber refresh timing is outside our control. Disabling a link removes its feed content from active service storage after the server confirms; a minimal revocation record remains to prevent an old request from restoring the feed. Offline revocation remains queued until a later connected run. Provider backups and security records follow Cloudflare’s retention practices. Subscribers may retain downloaded events until they remove the subscription.

Deleting, merging or moving a published calendar queues link revocation. Hiding it does not. Uninstalling this app does not revoke a published link; disable links first. With iCloud Sync on, a link’s management credential is kept in an encrypted field of your private iCloud data (end-to-end encrypted when Advanced Data Protection is on) so your other devices can update or disable it; with it off, the credential stays on the device that created the link and is not restored from backups, so contact support if access to an existing link is lost. The host necessarily processes IP addresses for connection security and rate limiting. Worker request logging is disabled; platform security processing may still apply. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Weather and holidays

When weather is enabled for a saved place, the app requests weather from Apple WeatherKit using that place’s coordinates. These are the selected weather city’s coordinates, not a reading of your device’s current location.

For holiday calendars you add, the app requests a country code and year from Nager.Date. Your event titles, notes and alarm names are not included in those requests. Internet service providers receive technical connection information such as your IP address. Their processing is governed by their own policies.

Optional travel monitoring

If you enable the travel setting, iOS location services can wake the app after significant changes so it can check the device’s time zone and reschedule alarms. The app ignores the coordinates supplied in those callbacks. It does not store or transmit a location history. You can turn this feature off in the app and manage its permission in iOS Settings.

Feedback and support

When you submit the feedback form, the app sends your feedback type, feature area, message and optional email address, along with the app version, build number, iOS version, device model and locale. The device model is a hardware model name, not a unique device identifier. A Google Apps Script receiver adds a submission timestamp and stores the report in a developer-managed Google Sheet.

We use this information to respond to support requests and diagnose app problems. Reports may be linked to you if you provide an email address or identifying details in your message. You do not need to provide an email address to send feedback. Please avoid including sensitive calendar, medical, financial or other personal information that is not needed for the request.

Feedback records are deleted two years after they are received. You can request earlier deletion using the contact above. Include the email address you used, or an approximate submission date and enough detail to identify the report. We may be unable to locate an anonymous report without that information. Hosting-provider backups and security records follow the provider’s retention practices.

If you email support directly, your address and message are used to handle your request. Google processes submitted in-app feedback as the hosting provider; see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, send an advertising identifier, or use your information for cross-app advertising tracking. We do not sell submitted feedback or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Your choices and deletion

You can remove records using the app’s controls, disconnect selected Apple calendars, disable weather and holiday features, and revoke permissions in iOS Settings. Deleting a record from the interface may leave internal deletion markers, recovery records or previously made backups; it is not a promise of immediate erasure from every storage layer. Removing the app removes its local app data subject to iOS shared-container and backup behavior. Offloading the app is different and may retain its data.

App removal does not delete your Apple Calendar account data or feedback already submitted to the developer. Manage system calendars through Apple Calendar or their account provider, and contact us for feedback deletion requests. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information; use the privacy contact above to make a request.

Legal basis

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom: we process feedback and support messages with your consent, and to pursue our legitimate interest in answering support requests and fixing problems. You can withdraw consent by asking us to delete your feedback.

Where data is processed

Feedback is stored with Google, and published calendar links with Cloudflare. They may process it in countries other than yours, including the United States.

Security and legal requests

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we receive, but no method of sending or storing data is completely secure. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their personal information. If a child has sent us information through feedback, contact us and we’ll delete it.

This website

These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising, or contact forms. GitHub Pages hosts them and may log technical connection information, including IP addresses, for security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Changes

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change and revise the date above. Calendar subscriptions are currently limited to explicitly enrolled development builds.
