Support

Help with Multi-Zone Clock.

Contact the developer

Email pureunhouse@gmail.com, or open Settings → Send Feedback in the app.

Describe what happened, what you expected, and the steps to reproduce it. Include the app and iOS versions if you email us. Please do not send passwords or private calendar details.

Alarms and reminders

Check that the alarm is enabled and that the app has the required permission. On supported iOS versions, AlarmKit alarms can ring through Silent mode. Notification fallback follows system notification settings. Calendar and holiday reminders scheduled by this app use a finite future window that refreshes when the app runs.

Apple calendars

Open Calendar → Manage Calendars to select system calendars. For unavailable calendars, check calendar permissions and whether the calendar still exists in Apple Calendar. Disconnect removes the app’s connection; it leaves Apple Calendar data intact.

For an interrupted move, follow the recovery prompt. Check Again checks the existing write’s result; it does not repeat an uncertain write. Avoid creating another copy while recovery is unresolved.

Dates and time zones

View In changes how events appear in another time zone; it does not rewrite their stored times. Holiday conversion has its own setting. A date shift across distant time zones can be expected when conversion is enabled.

Privacy requests

To request access to or deletion of submitted feedback, email the contact above. Include your submission email or approximate date and message details so the record can be found. See the Privacy Policy for local data, connected calendars and retention.

More help

The app’s Settings → Help explains time zones, alarms, calendars and connected-calendar behavior. Direct Google Calendar API sync and public calendar subscription links are not currently offered.
